# Evidence supporting the rescue map

Reviewed 22 October 2026. This is a bounded primary-source review, not a systematic review. No cited study establishes rescue of the accepted L737*/N1002K genotype.

| Source | Supported observation | Limit for this project |
| --- | --- | --- |
| [Suijkerbuijk et al., 2010](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC2887387/) | BUBR1 mutant functions can separate; ectopic wild-type protein restored checkpoint activity in other MVA-derived cells | Other genotypes and cell endpoints; not whole-body efficacy |
| [Lueck et al., 2019, ACE-tRNAs](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC6379413/) | Screen included leucine suppressor tRNAs targeting UGA | Reported Gly/Trp fidelity measurements do not verify Leu incorporation at BUB1B L737* |
| [Pierce et al., 2025, PERT](https://www.nature.com/articles/s41586-025-09732-2) | Endogenous screening identified TGA suppression with leucine and arginine backbones; optimized therapeutic demonstrations chiefly concern TAG | Do not transfer UAG/TAG efficacy, fidelity or safety to UGA/TGA or BUB1B; screens differed in delivery and expression |
| [Xu et al., 2026, AAV-NoSTOP](https://www.nature.com/articles/s41587-025-02982-5) | Lead UGA suppressor predominantly inserted Arg in the assayed substrate | Would primarily imply a hypothetical L737R product here, not native Leu; proportions cannot be assumed in BUB1B |
| [Roy et al., 2016](https://doi.org/10.1073/pnas.1605336113) | Readthrough can produce context-dependent amino-acid mixtures | Does not identify the mixture or competence of BUB1B products |
| [Cox et al., 2017, REPAIR](https://doi.org/10.1126/science.aaq0180) | Programmable ADAR-mediated A-to-I RNA editing | UGA to UGG is sequence reasoning here; no exact-target editing efficiency established |
| [Anzalone et al., 2019, prime editing](https://www.nature.com/articles/s41586-019-1711-4) | Platform can install all classes of point substitution | No BUB1B guide, efficiency, byproduct or delivery result is established |
| [C-to-G editing study, 2025](https://doi.org/10.1093/nar/gkaf717) | C:G to G:C transversion editing is a distinct class beyond standard CBE/ABE | Complementary-strand editing could in principle make AAG to AAC; PAM/window and safety are untested here |
| [Transversion-editing byproduct study, 2024](https://www.nature.com/articles/s41556-023-01342-2) | C-to-G editing can generate DNA breaks and structural byproducts in tested systems | A reason to characterize byproducts; not a measured risk rate for this genotype |
| [Kim et al., 2022, gene-specific NMD inhibition](https://www.nature.com/articles/s41467-022-30668-y) | EJC-directed ASOs increased CFTR-W1282X expression and channel function; splicing changes occurred | CFTR truncated-protein activity does not transfer to BubR1 1-736; BUB1B has several downstream junctions |

The exon-17 result is a new computational inference from the public reference, not a published rescue claim: the exact modeled skip removes 141 coding bases, produces a 1,003-residue protein and is sequence-equivalent to deletion of Glu715-Leu761. It must be assessed both with and without N1002K. Predicted reading-frame preservation does not establish protein folding, interactions or chromosome fidelity.

The prior ataluren nomination and its broader negative evidence remain in the original local research workspace. This new repository does not replace that review with a stronger efficacy claim. It asks which protein-restoration hypothesis can survive direct functional testing.
