# Research priority: identify the product that can rescue division

The most useful contribution is a decision package a laboratory can act on: which protein needs to be restored, what stops it being made, and which result would justify or rule out each approach. The earlier approved-drug review already covered 16 compounds and extensively audited ataluren. Repeating protein prediction scores would not resolve its central uncertainties.

Track 1 is complete: compound-heterozygous BUB1B is the accepted diagnosis, reconfirmed by the participant on 22 October 2026. The rescue analysis uses the established project pair, L737*/N1002K in trans. The remaining work concerns protein function and intervention feasibility. Recommendations below concern research priorities, not treatment selection or readiness.

## First establish the functional benchmark

Ask **whether restoring a controlled amount of native BubR1 improves chromosome-segregation fidelity in the relevant genotype**, and whether the N1002K protein retains useful activity at comparable abundance. Wild-type restoration has precedent in other BUB1B disease-cell models, but does not establish rescue of these alleles, affected organs or developmental injury. [Disease-cell experiments](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC2887387/)

Use accurate completed divisions as the primary concept for rescue. Interpret RNA abundance, full-length protein, checkpoint behavior, mitotic timing, cell death and clonal growth together. A population can appear healthier because abnormal cells died, stopped dividing or were outgrown. Neither full-length protein nor increased proliferation alone answers the question.

## Research branches in priority order

| Branch | What it could restore | What would make it worth pursuing | What remains unresolved |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Native BUB1B correction or controlled wild-type transcript/gene replacement | Native protein, avoiding assumed tolerance of new residues | A reproducible functional benchmark and reversible phenotype | Delivery, tissue coverage, regulation, duration, editing byproducts and oncological effects |
| UGA-directed leucine suppressor tRNA | Potentially native Leu737 from the stop copy, retaining native Asn1002 | Correct amino-acid identity and useful endogenous protein | Native-context activity, RNA availability, fidelity, normal-stop effects and delivery; no BUB1B rescue shown |
| Defined L737W/R/C products, compared with native protein | Different full-length proteins | Product competence established independently of drug yield | Existing prediction scores cannot establish function |
| N1002K protein protection | More of the existing missense product | Retained function at matched abundance plus evidence of limiting stability | Neither requirement has been measured for this allele |
| Exon-17 skipping | A 47-residue deletion product | Functional shortened protein and a favorable combined-allele effect | No function established; changing the other allele could reduce residual function |
| Gene-specific protection from NMD | More stop-bearing transcript | Demonstrated RNA limitation plus a functional protein-restoration partner | Not a stand-alone rescue; truncated protein, altered splicing and downstream junctions remain concerns |

This order prioritizes informative comparisons. It does not mean gene therapy is more clinically ready than repurposed drugs.

Exon 17 is a useful new hypothesis, not the strongest efficacy claim. Its deletion retains the annotated kinase domain but removes part of a region used in CENP-E interaction experiments, including the position of the impaired R727C comparator. Preserving domain labels does not prove preserved interactions. The deletion-plus-N1002K product retains Lys at new residue 955, corresponding to reference position 1002. [Mutant-function study](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC2887387/)

## Decisions that would change the next step

| Observation | Consequence |
| --- | --- |
| Native-protein restoration improves segregation, but L737W/R/C products do not | Favor native-residue approaches; deprioritize readthrough strategies yielding those products |
| A nonnative full-length product works, but endogenous treatment produces too little | Focus on RNA availability, production and exposure rather than more structure scoring |
| N1002K works at matched abundance and is unstable in its native context | Protein protection gains a concrete mechanistic basis |
| N1002K remains defective at adequate abundance | More missense protein is unlikely to be sufficient by itself |
| Exon-17 deletion works on the stop copy but harms the missense copy | Nonselective skipping is not validated; allele selectivity or another route becomes decisive |
| Apparent improvement comes from reduced division or selective cell loss | Do not count it as restoration of segregation fidelity |
| Native restoration fails in the chosen model | Reassess model validity, expression, endpoint and reversibility before inferring that the disease is unrescuable |

These are proposed comparisons, not completed experiments. No numerical rescue threshold or success probability is supplied because the necessary measurements do not exist here. Subsequent tissue and tumor-relevant work would be needed to assess benefit and risk beyond a short nonmalignant-cell assay.

## What is feasible before the final

The public-reference sequence audit, product map, source-linked comparisons and reproducible code are completed in this repository. They expose practical differences among strategies and identify an in-frame exon-skipping hypothesis requiring a stringent functional check. RNA abundance, N1002K activity, drug response and the required amount of rescue cannot be recovered from the available DNA reads. The next useful handoff is this decision package with those unknowns explicit.

The earlier ataluren proposal remains a bounded hypothesis. Its negative evidence and rejection criteria should travel with it. The broader research contribution is to make native-protein restoration the benchmark against which that hypothesis and emerging approaches are judged.
